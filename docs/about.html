About this project

Multimodal Video Generator using Gemini is an open-source exploration of that philosophy.

It brings together generative video, image, voice, music, and intelligent orchestration into a structured creative pipeline. Built around Google Gemini and Vertex AI, the project emphasizes reproducibility, brand consistency, cost-aware execution, and human approval before expensive generation steps.

My goal is to make advanced multimodal AI workflows more accessible to developers, creators, and innovators while demonstrating how production-oriented agentic engineering principles can transform creative workflows.
